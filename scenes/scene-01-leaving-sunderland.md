# Scene 1 — Leaving Sunderland

**Who appears:** Norah (brief possible glimpse of her mother)  
**Where:** Norah’s home (terrace of large middle-class Victorian houses in Sunderland) → walk to the station → ticket office → platform → train  

**What happens:**  
Norah leaves home on a bright morning, walks to the station, buys her ticket, and boards the train for the long journey. She carries a book.  

**How it moves the story on:**  
Establishes Norah’s world, her stylish independence, and the beginning of the journey. Voice-over (descendant, How I Met Your Mother style) introduces the family origin story.  

**Notes:**  
Sets the 1924 period and Norah’s fashionable presentation (bob haircut).
