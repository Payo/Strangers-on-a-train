# Scene 4 — The Long Conversation

**Who appears:** Norah, James  
**Where:** Compartment of the Bristol train; brief stop at Durham  

**What happens:**  
The train stops at Durham. Norah looks out at the cathedral. James re-enters, a little embarrassed, carrying a newspaper (his excuse for having left). He sits opposite. They begin talking. Hours pass — changing light, stations, the book set aside.  

**How it moves the story on:**  
Establishes the real connection. James’s return shows he has overcome the flight response. The long conversation creates the bond that makes the later separation painful and the advert necessary.  

**Notes:**  
Durham cathedral visible from the window. Newspaper as Chekhov’s gun / practical excuse. Time passing shown visually rather than through dialogue.
