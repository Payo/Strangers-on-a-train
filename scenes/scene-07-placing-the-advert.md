# Scene 7 — Placing the Advert

**Who appears:** James, telegraph clerk  
**Where:** Post office, Bristol  

**What happens:**  
James dictates the advert to the telegraph clerk: a request for anyone who knows the young woman with blue eyes and a bobbed haircut who travelled from Sunderland to Bridgwater on 4 April at 8:15 a.m. to contact James Pillans at the Red Lion Hotel, Bristol. The clerk smirks slightly. James is mildly embarrassed but presses on.  

**How it moves the story on:**  
The decisive act of courage. Turns private determination into a public, irreversible step. Creates the means by which Norah’s mother will later find the message.  

**Notes:**  
Period post-office atmosphere. The slight embarrassment keeps James human and modest.
