# Scene 6 — James at the Red Lion

**Who appears:** James, friend  
**Where:** The Red Lion Hotel bar, Bristol  

**What happens:**  
James tells the story of the train meeting to a friend over a drink. As he recounts it, his determination grows.  

**How it moves the story on:**  
Shows the internal decision forming. The friend is a sounding board; the advert becomes inevitable.  

**Notes:**  
Warm, ordinary hotel bar. The shift from light storytelling to quiet resolve is the key beat.
