# Scene 8 — Mother Sees the Advert

**Who appears:** Norah’s mother  
**Where:** Family home in Sunderland  

**What happens:**  
The newspaper is delivered through the door. Norah’s mother picks it up, sits down to read, and notices the small advert describing the young woman with blue eyes and a bob who travelled from Sunderland to Bridgwater. She smiles and goes to the telephone to call Norah.  

**How it moves the story on:**  
The public message reaches the right person. Creates the bridge between James’s act of courage and Norah’s later decision.  

**Notes:**  
Ordinary domestic morning. The smile is knowing and affectionate.
