# Scene 5 — Bristol: Norah Jumps Out

**Who appears:** Norah, James  
**Where:** Compartment and platform at Bristol  

**What happens:**  
The train stops. Norah is mid-conversation. She looks up, sees the station sign “Bristol,” grabs her suitcase, opens the door and steps off, saying simply “good-bye, lovely to meet you.” The train pulls away. Only then does James realise he does not know her name or address (palm-face moment).  

**How it moves the story on:**  
Creates the central problem. The connection has been made, but the practical means of continuing it has been lost. Sets up James’s later decision to place the advert.  

**Notes:**  
Norah’s departure is brisk and practical, not dramatic. James’s realisation is delayed until the train is moving again.
