# Scene 2 — Changing Trains at Newcastle

**Who appears:** Norah  
**Where:** Newcastle Station — platforms, concourse, Bristol train  

**What happens:**  
Norah alights from the Sunderland train. The station is busy and noisy with people shouting as they find their connections. She crosses the platform and boards the train to Bristol, placing her bags on the rack opposite so she can see them. She sits and opens her book.  

**How it moves the story on:**  
Moves her onto the correct train for the meeting. The bustle and noise heighten the sense of ordinary travel just before the extraordinary encounter. Builds quiet anticipation.  

**Notes:**  
Period station atmosphere — steam, crowds, shouting.
