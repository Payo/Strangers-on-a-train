# Scene 10 — Tea and Cake in the Garden

**Who appears:** Norah, James, Norah’s mother  
**Where:** Norah’s family home in Sunderland — front door and garden  

**What happens:**  
James knocks at the Victorian terrace. Norah’s mother invites him in. Norah is in the garden. She and James meet there and resume the easy conversation they began on the train, now over tea and cake.  

**How it moves the story on:**  
Resolves the central problem. The connection made on the train is continued in ordinary domestic life.  

**Closing voice-over (fade out):**  
James and Norah got married, had a daughter Felicity, who had three children, eight grandchildren, and five great-great-grandchildren.  

**Notes:**  
Warm, unforced reunion. The garden setting keeps it intimate and true to the family home established in Scene 1. The voice-over continues over the fade.
