# Scene 3 — James Boards and Leaves

**Who appears:** Norah, James  
**Where:** Compartment of the Bristol train at Newcastle Station  

**What happens:**  
Just before departure, James (fair hair, glasses) enters the compartment. He places his suitcases on the rack above Norah’s seat, turns, and smiles at her. Then, overwhelmed, he pats his jacket as if he has lost or forgotten something and steps back out of the carriage. The train begins to pull away. Norah watches, puzzled.  

**How it moves the story on:**  
Introduces James and establishes the flight response. Creates the first small mystery and the seed of the later problem (he will return, but the moment of hesitation has already occurred).  

**Notes:**  
Steam, smoke, and station noise outside the window. Displacement activity is deliberate and visible.
