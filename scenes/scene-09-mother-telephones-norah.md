# Scene 9 — Mother Telephones Norah

**Who appears:** Norah, Norah’s mother (on the telephone)  
**Where:** Cousin’s house near Bridgwater (Norah); family home in Sunderland (mother)  

**What happens:**  
Norah’s mother telephones and reads the advert aloud (James’s home address in Newcastle). Norah laughs and says she isn’t interested. After the call, her mother contacts James and invites him to tea and cake with Norah when she returns from her holiday.  

**How it moves the story on:**  
Creates a gentle tension between Norah’s spoken refusal and her mother’s quiet initiative. Sets up the final meeting without Norah having to make the first move.  

**Notes:**  
Norah is still on holiday. The invitation is arranged by the mother for Norah’s return.
