# Platform-Ready Video Shot List — Norah and James

~12-minute short film. Each shot is designed for 5–15 second AI video generations (or up to 30 s where platforms allow). Use the corresponding reference images from the reference-image-pack for consistency.

Voice-over runs continuously (How I Met Your Mother style). Generate picture with light ambience; add VO in edit.

## Scene 1 — Leaving Sunderland
**Shot 1.1** (8 s)  
Wide shot of Norah stepping out of the Victorian terrace house on a bright summer morning. She carries a leather suitcase and a book. Camera slowly pushes in.  
**Prompt:** Wide shot of a stylish young woman in 1924 with a dark bob haircut leaving a Victorian terrace house in Sunderland. She carries a brown leather suitcase and a book. Bright summer morning light, soft natural movement. Period photographic realism.

**Shot 1.2** (6 s)  
Medium tracking shot of Norah walking along the street toward the station.  
**Prompt:** Medium tracking shot of a stylish young woman in 1924 walking along a Sunderland street carrying a suitcase. Bright summer light, gentle camera movement. Period photographic realism.

## Scene 2 — Changing Trains at Newcastle
**Shot 2.1** (10 s)  
Busy Newcastle station platform. Norah alights and crosses toward the Bristol train amid steam and crowds.  
**Prompt:** Busy 1924 steam railway station platform at Newcastle. Crowds, porters, thick smoke and steam. A stylish young woman with a dark bob crosses the platform carrying a suitcase. Atmospheric daylight. Period photographic realism.

**Shot 2.2** (6 s)  
Norah boards the Bristol train and places her suitcase on the rack.  
**Prompt:** Interior of a 1924 railway compartment. A stylish young woman places a leather suitcase on the luggage rack and sits down. Soft daylight through the window. Period photographic realism.

## Scene 3 — James Boards and Leaves
**Shot 3.1** (8 s)  
James enters the compartment, places his cases, smiles at Norah.  
**Prompt:** 1924 railway compartment. A handsome young man with fair hair and round wire-frame glasses enters, places suitcases on the rack, and smiles at a woman seated opposite. Soft daylight. Period photographic realism.

**Shot 3.2** (6 s)  
James pats his jacket (displacement), steps off. Train begins to move. Norah watches, puzzled.  
**Prompt:** 1924 railway compartment. The young man pats his jacket as if he has forgotten something, then steps out. The train begins to pull away. The woman watches, puzzled. Steam and station noise outside. Period photographic realism.

## Scene 4 — The Long Conversation
**Shot 4.1** (8 s)  
Train stops at Durham. Norah looks out at the cathedral. James re-enters with a newspaper.  
**Prompt:** 1924 railway compartment stopped at Durham. A woman looks out at the cathedral. A young man re-enters carrying a newspaper and sits opposite, slightly embarrassed. Soft daylight. Period photographic realism.

**Shot 4.2** (12 s)  
They talk. Time passes (changing light, stations).  
**Prompt:** 1924 railway compartment. A young woman with a dark bob and a young man with glasses talk easily opposite each other. Light changes, countryside passes. Gentle camera drift. Period photographic realism.

## Scene 5 — Bristol: Norah Jumps Out
**Shot 5.1** (8 s)  
Train stops at Bristol. Norah sees the sign, grabs her suitcase, steps off with a brief goodbye.  
**Prompt:** 1924 railway compartment at Bristol. A woman looks up, sees the station sign, grabs her suitcase and steps off saying goodbye. Soft daylight. Period photographic realism.

**Shot 5.2** (6 s)  
Train pulls away. James realises he has no name or address (palm-face).  
**Prompt:** 1924 railway compartment. The train pulls away from Bristol. A young man with glasses realises he does not know the woman’s name, palm to face. Soft daylight. Period photographic realism.

## Scene 6 — James at the Red Lion

James in the Red Lion bar with a friend, telling the story. Determination grows.  
**Prompt:** Warm 1924 hotel bar interior with etched glass and dark wood. A young man with glasses tells a story to a friend; his expression shifts from light to determined. Warm chandelier light. Period photographic realism.

James at the post-office counter dictating the advert to the clerk.  
**Prompt:** 1924 post-office interior. A young man with round wire-frame glasses stands at the wooden telegraph counter dictating a message to a clerk. Ordinary daytime light. Period photographic realism.

## Scene 8 — Mother Sees the Advert

Newspaper delivered. Mother picks it up, reads, notices the advert, smiles.  
**Prompt:** 1924 Sunderland home interior. An older woman picks up a delivered newspaper, sits, reads, and smiles at a small advert. Soft morning light. Period photographic realism.

## Scene 9 — Mother Telephones Norah
**Shot 9.1** (8 s)  
Mother on the telephone reading the advert. (Norah’s reaction can be a separate short shot or implied.)  
**Prompt:** 1924 home interior. An older woman speaks on a period telephone, reading from a newspaper, smiling. Soft indoor light. Period photographic realism.

## Scene 10 — Tea and Cake in the Garden
**Shot 10.1** (6 s)  
James knocks at the front door. Mother invites him in.  
**Prompt:** Exterior of a Victorian terrace house in Sunderland. A young man with glasses knocks at the door. An older woman opens it and invites him in. Bright summer light. Period photographic realism.

**Shot 10.2** (12 s)  
Garden. Norah and James meet and resume conversation over tea and cake. Fade out with closing VO.  
**Prompt:** Modest 1924 back garden. A young woman with a dark bob and a young man with glasses sit at a wooden table with tea, cake and scones, talking easily. Bright summer light, greenery, brick house behind. Gentle camera drift, then slow fade. Period photographic realism.
