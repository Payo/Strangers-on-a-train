# Logline

In 1924, a missed name and a newspaper advert turn a nine-hour train conversation into a lifelong romance — retold by a descendant as a gentle counterpoint to modern dating.

## Agreed Decisions

- **Length**: Approximately 12 minutes.
- **Tone**: Romantic core with funny elements. Warm, gentle, and slightly comic. Explicit contrast with modern dating culture.
- **Framing**: Modern re-telling of the family origin story. Light present-day frame (descendant reflecting) bookends the 1924 action.
- **Modern dating contrast**: Lives mainly in the modern frame. 1924 scenes stay pure; no heavy-handed commentary inside the period action.
- **Central emotional question**: What does it take for two strangers to recognise each other when the only tools are attention, time, and a little courage?
- **Source**: True family story of Norah and James Pillans meeting on a train in 1924.
