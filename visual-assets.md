# Visual Assets

## Norah’s Sunderland House and Garden

**Description / Image Prompt**  
A large middle-class Victorian terrace house in Sunderland, 1924, inspired by period North-East terraces. Red brick or stone, sash windows, bay windows, neat front garden and steps. Bright summer morning light. Later, a modest back garden with a small table set for tea and cake.

**Notes**  
Used in Scene 1 (departure) and Scene 10 (tea and cake reunion).  
Images provided by producer as reference only — not stored.

## Newcastle and Bristol Station Platforms

**Description / Image Prompt**  
Busy 1924 steam railway station platform. Crowds of passengers, porters shouting, thick smoke and steam from locomotives. Period clothing. Newcastle or Bristol station atmosphere.

**Notes**  
Used in Scene 2 (Newcastle change) and Scene 5 (Bristol departure). Steam and smoke are essential.
